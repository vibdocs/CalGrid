# Important Limits & Boundaries

CalGrid uses Google Calendar and Google Drive through your own account. Keep these boundaries in mind:

- **Delivery is not guaranteed.** “Done” means an API request completed; guests may not receive an email or accept an event.
- **Google controls quotas and policies.** Fast/Ultra modes and randomized timing do not bypass rate limits or anti-abuse checks.
- **Stop is not recall.** A batch already accepted by Google cannot be unsent from the CalGrid button.
- **Licenses and Google accounts are separate.** Both must be authorized. CalGrid's license may require an active internet connection.
- **Google Drive attachments** depend on file permissions, sharing rules and Drive API access.
- **Meet links and reminders** depend on Google account/Calendar capabilities; reminders are not necessarily attendee alerts.
- **Windows x64 only.** CalGrid is not a Mac, Linux or mobile app.

Use CalGrid responsibly with people who expect your invitations. Check private file sharing and avoid exposing personal data in event descriptions.
