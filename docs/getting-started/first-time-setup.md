# First-Time Setup

## Activate your license

1. Open **CalGrid.exe**.
2. On the **License** screen, enter your valid license key.
3. Choose **Verify / Login** with an active internet connection.
4. After successful verification, CalGrid opens your workspaces in the same window.

CalGrid does not unlock the workspace until the license service authorizes the session. Follow the message on screen if activation fails.

## Connect Google Calendar

In the left **Google Auth** section, choose **+ Add** to select your OAuth desktop JSON file if needed, then choose **Connect**. Sign in with your Google account in the browser and approve the requested permissions.

Your CalGrid license and Google sign-in are separate. Do not share license keys, OAuth client files or saved tokens.

## Prepare your first invitation

Enter an event title, date and time, description and recipient emails. Select **Live Preview** before starting a campaign.
