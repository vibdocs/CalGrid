# Sending Modes

Choose the sending mode in the workspace before you start.

| Mode | Typical purpose |
|---|---|
| **Safe** | More conservative pacing |
| **Normal** | Balanced everyday sending |
| **Fast** | Faster processing when your Google quota allows |
| **Ultra** | Highest requested pace; greater risk of quota errors |

Modes change pacing; they **do not bypass Google quotas, guarantee inbox delivery or guarantee spam avoidance**. All workspaces share rate-limit safeguards.

For new accounts or uncertain limits, begin conservatively and check the [progress panel](progress-monitoring.md). Adjust request pacing and retry settings in [Settings](../settings/application-settings.md).
