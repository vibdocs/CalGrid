# Start, Pause, Resume & Stop

Use the controls in your workspace after connecting Google, entering event details and checking recipients.

- **Start:** Begin sending eligible invitations for the current workspace.
- **Pause:** Hold subsequent work while keeping the job's progress.
- **Resume:** Continue a paused job.
- **Stop:** Request that the worker stop preparing and sending new batches.

You may see some work finish after Pause or Stop: a request already in flight to Google cannot be unsent. Do not press Start repeatedly to retry uncertain results.

Buttons may be unavailable while Google is disconnected, the license is not authorized or the workspace is finishing another job.

Watch [Progress Monitoring](progress-monitoring.md) for success/failure counts and consult [Debug](debug-dialog.md) for errors.
