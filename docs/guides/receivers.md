# Recipients

Recipients are the email addresses invited to your event.

## Add addresses

- Type or paste addresses into the recipient area, separated by lines or common delimiters.
- Choose **Upload List** to import a supported file, such as CSV, TXT or Excel.
- You can also drag a supported list file onto the recipient area.

CalGrid scans imported lists and shows the resulting email count. Use **Remove Duplicates** to avoid repeated addresses, or **Clear** to start again.

**Important:** Verify the address list before starting. Avoid including anyone who did not expect an invitation. Removing duplicates from a list does not guarantee delivery.

Successful imports contribute to local history/accounting; a cancelled or failed import should not be treated as a successful campaign.
